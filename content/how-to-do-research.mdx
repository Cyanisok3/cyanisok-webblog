## How to Search for Reference Papers?

- **Top Conference Papers**<br />
  ML: ICML, NeurlPS, ICLR<br />
  NLP: ACL, EMNLP, COLM<br />
  CV: CVPR, ICCV, ECCV<br />
  AI: AAAI, IJCAI<br />
  Data Mining & Info Retrieval: KDD<br />
  Multidisciplinary: TPAMI, JMLR, TMLR, TKDE, Nature Machine Intelligence, CHI, etc.
- **Seed Papers**<br />
  High quality surveys published recently<br />
  Widely acknowledged representative works<br />
  Papers that are highly related to your RQ<br />
  Widely used Benchmark papers<br />
  Top conference && Representative papers
- **Paper citation network**<br />
  forward<br />
  backward
- **Following some top authors / research groups**<br />
  Personal websites<br />
  Google Scholar, DBLP, Semantic Scholar pages<br />
  Lab homepage of some research groups<br />
  Github page, project page, Hugging Face page<br />
  <u>Social media platforms</u> (WeChat, RedNote, etc.)
- **Find their code, dataset or benchmark first**<br />
  Official websites<br />
  Leaderboard<br />
  Hugging Face<br />
  Github<br />
  Compared methods cited in their papers

## How to Read Papers?

1. **Go through Abstract, pictures and charts**<br />
   Who?<br />
   Where does it published?<br />
   What’s the problem?<br />
   What’s the effect / achievement?
2. **What’s the RQ?**<br />
   Find it in the Abstract or Introduction (However, our method / we propose…)
3. **Why do they need to solve it?**<br />
   Precedent research/papers?<br />
   Meaningful?<br />
   Who else done it?<br />
   Any other solutions?<br />
   Any challenges/limitations in the remained solutions?<br />
   How would I deal with it?
4. **Read its Methodology**<br />
   Critical thinking<br />
   Limitations?<br />
   Can I do better?
5. **Analyze & Reproduce the experiment**<br />
   Which <u>dataset</u> it used?<br />
   What are the <u>compared methods</u>?<br />
   <u>Why</u> them?<br />
   Is the <u>experiment design</u> reasonable?<br />
   Is the <u>conclusion</u> reasonable?<br />
   Any <u>flaws</u>?<br />
   Available <u>open-source code</u>?
6. **Find its related works**
7. Reflections<br />
   Critical thinking & Creative thinking

## How to come up with a Plausible Idea?

### Problem-driven research

e.g.:

<div style={{ paddingLeft: '2em' }}>
  How to **improve** the model’s performance?<br />
  Define a new **benchmark**?<br />
  **Analyze** the existed methods based on existed benchmark?
</div>

### Method-driven research

Using **existed method** to solve a <u>new problem</u> / apply (adapt) it to a <u>new field</u>

> We can also combine the two paradigms above

### How to find RQs, though?

- **Being overly nitpicky**<br />
  Raise up questions at the time you reading a paper
- **Find your chance in its failure cases**<br />
  <u>Find</u> and <u>reproduce</u> them, then <u>analyze</u> why and try to <u>resolve</u> it
- **Reverse Engineering:** Infer its mechanizm from its performance<br />
  If it’s effective but lack of explanation, ask ‘how does it actually work?’<br />
  Then you may need to design experiments to analyze it based on coding
- **Similar experiment, opposite results**<br />
  Why the results of experiement A and B are different? ‘Cause of data? Assumptions?<br />
  Benchmark?
- **Find research problems in your internships**

### How to evaluate if an idea is worth doing?

Talk with your supervisors / seniors

### How to resolve a research problem?

- **Migrate** these mature/validated existed methods<br />
  read a bunch of papers, seek techniques with identical problem core
- Can we **integrate** method A into B?
- **Create** a new method, **refactor** the problem
- **AI-assisted** inspirations

## How to Conduct Meeting with Supervisor Effectively?

- **Expose** encountered problems
- **Clarify** & **Organize** your thoughts
- Get **feedback**s
- Carry on~
